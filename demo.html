ReKod Chrome extension + dashboard · v0.2 · for the whole team

Show the bug once. Never explain it again.

Press one shortcut and ReKod records your screen. It quietly attaches everything a developer would ask for — the errors, the network calls, your browser and device — even from the five minutes before you hit record. You get one link anyone can open. No DevTools, no long write-up, no back-and-forth.

⌥ Option⇧ ShiftJ on any page in Chrome — that's the whole app

On Windows or Linux it's Alt Shift J. You can change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

The problem

Everyone has sent “it's broken on my machine.”

A proper bug report takes fifteen minutes. Make it happen again, open DevTools, screenshot the errors, copy the failed request, note your browser and screen size, write the steps. So nobody does it. We send “the pricing page looks weird” — and the questions start.

The bug is gone by the time you record

The error already happened. Getting it to happen again is the slow part — and sometimes it just won't.

Two days of “can you try again?”

What browser? Any errors? Can you send a screenshot? Every question costs a day and an interruption on both sides.

You shouldn't need to be a developer

Designers spot broken layouts. Support hears about failed checkouts. Whoever sees the problem should be able to report it properly.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

ReKod is already watching

As soon as a page opens, ReKod keeps the last five minutes of what happened behind the scenes. It stays in your browser — nothing is sent until you choose to share.

Press the shortcut

⌥ Option ⇧ Shift J, or click the icon. Record the tab, or grab a screenshot and crop it. Most recordings take under a minute.

Add a sentence and send

Say what went wrong — or skip it, it's optional. Your report is uploaded and ready to share before you've switched tabs.

In every report

Everything a developer would ask you for. Already there.

See what happened before you hit record the big one

Errors and requests from up to five minutes earlier. They show with a minus sign — −0:12 means twelve seconds before you started — so the cause is right there.

A video of exactly what you saw

Your tab, up to three minutes. Files stay small (about 10 MB), so the link opens instantly.

Every error, warning and log

The full console, with stack traces. Warnings count as errors here, because they usually are.

Every network call, with the data

What was sent, what came back and how long it took — neatly formatted and colour-coded. Live (WebSocket) messages too.

Click an error, jump to the moment the magic trick

Video and logs stay in sync. Click a log line and the video jumps there; scrub the video and the logs follow. Red markers show exactly where things broke.

Browser and device details, automatically

Browser, OS, screen size, graphics card and build version. Nobody has to ask, nobody has to guess.

Passwords and tokens stay private

Logins, cookies and tokens are removed inside your browser before anything uploads. You'll see ✓ redacted before you hit send. Always on.

Screenshots, cropped in one drag

Drag a box around what matters, or click once for the whole tab. Done.

One link. No sign-up needed.

Send it to anyone — a teammate, a contractor, a client on their phone. Add ?view=media to share just the video.

Comments right on the report

Add a title and notes whenever you like. The conversation lives next to the evidence, not lost in a chat thread.

Find any report in seconds

All your reports in one place, with thumbnails. Search, filter by project or date, and clean up several at once.

Your recordings stay with us

Everything is stored in our own database, not on someone else's servers. Each report belongs to the person who made it, and no one can peek around that.

Who it's for

For anyone who's ever said “it's broken.”

Developers get all the technical detail for free. Everyone else just records what they see — and gets taken seriously the first time.

Product, support & ops

Report it without writing it up

  • The recording is the report. No steps to write. One sentence is enough.
  • Flag what feels slow, not just what breaks. A nine-second checkout shows up as nine seconds of waiting — with the slow request right underneath.
  • Share outside the team. Clients, agencies and vendors can open the link. No account, no login.
Design

Show the glitch, not a description of it

  • Motion is captured. Hover states, animations and layout jumps show up on video. A screenshot loses all three.
  • Crop to the exact component. Drag, send. No screenshot tool, no files piling up on your desktop.
  • Clean review links. ?view=media shows just the video, nothing technical.
  • No more “works on my screen.” Screen size and resolution are in every report.
QA & engineering

The clues you didn't know to capture

  • Five minutes of hindsight. The error that fired before anyone hit record is already there.
  • Real data, not screenshots of data. Full request and response bodies, next to the exact frame.
  • Skip the triage questions. Browser, OS, GPU and build — answered before anyone asks.

Try it today

Your first ReKod in four minutes.

  1. Go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the extension/ folder.
  2. Open the dashboard and sign in. The extension uses the same login — nothing else to set up.
  3. Open staging and break something. Wait for an error to show up.
  4. Press ⌥ Option ⇧ Shift J, record for about 20 seconds, type one sentence, hit send.
  5. Your report opens in a new tab. Click a red error line — the video jumps to that moment. Copy the link and share it anywhere.

Try this on your first report: scroll to the top of the logs and look for a time with a minus sign. That happened before you even started recording.
